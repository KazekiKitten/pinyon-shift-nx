# Pinyon Shift 0.5.0

Native builds for Linux, the Steam Deck and Apple silicon Macs, your own DLC
and the optional title update v4 build, HDR output, your own music and radio
stations, and updates from inside the launcher.

Each launcher still builds the game on your machine from your own disc; none
of the downloads contains the game. The site's [Features](https://arcanite24.github.io/pinyon-shift/features/)
page shows what each platform supports.

## New platforms

- **Linux and the Steam Deck**, natively rather than through Proton.
  Download `PinyonShift-Launcher-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, choose your disc
  image and **Verify and build**, then **Add to Steam** to play from Game
  Mode. The race and free roam hold 60 fps at 1x under the Deck's default
  15 W limit. On Linux, **F6 > GRAPHICS > SCENE SHADING > COARSE** saves
  about 8 % of the Deck's GPU frame for a slightly softer image. See
  [Linux and Steam Deck](../LINUX.md).
- **macOS on Apple silicon** (M1 or newer, macOS 13 or newer). Vulkan runs
  on Metal through MoltenVK, which the build includes. Download
  `PinyonShift-Launcher-macos-arm64.zip`; the app is not notarized, so
  approve its first start in **System Settings > Privacy & Security**. Free
  roam runs at a median of about 105 fps at 1x on an M4 Pro. See
  [macOS](../MACOS.md).
- **Android (alpha, for developers)** installs over Wi-Fi: the Windows
  launcher's **Android > Share on Wi-Fi** shows a QR code and a pairing
  code, and the app copies the game files, title update, DLC and optionally
  your save from the PC. USB debugging is no longer needed. The app id is now
  `studio.deimos.pinyonshift`; `python tools/pinyon.py android migrate`
  moves an earlier install's saves and files. See [Android](../ANDROID.md).

## New

- **DLC from your own packages.** The launcher's **DLC** panel verifies
  each Xbox 360 package against a catalog of 21 *Forza Horizon* packages
  and imports it. The monthly car packs are qualified; Horizon Rally plays
  from **F6** on the base build. Windows only for now.
- **Title update v4 build (optional).** Built from your disc and your own
  copy of the update, it runs the original Horizon Rally and the 1000 Club
  car challenges offline. A save written by it can't go back to the base
  build; the launcher backs the save up first. Windows and Android.
- **HDR output** on HDR displays (**F6 > DISPLAY > HDR**), with HDR PAPER
  WHITE, HDR HIGHLIGHTS, a calibration screen and an SDR and HDR side by
  side comparison. It switches without a restart.
- **Your music.** **F6 > AUDIO > YOUR MUSIC** plays your MP3 and WAV files
  from the `music` folder once the festival has loaded, silencing the radio
  as an Xbox 360 dashboard soundtrack did. <kbd>F9</kbd> skips.
- **Custom radio stations.** The launcher's **Radio** panel turns your music
  into up to three stations, each with its own name, logo and songs, in place
  of Bass Arena, Pulse or Rocks. It needs FFmpeg.
- **Immersive camera** (optional, **F6 > DISPLAY > IMMERSIVE CAMERA**):
  G-forces, lean into corners, speed shake and head motion, with presets and
  separate amounts, applied at once.
- **Update from the launcher.** **Update to <version>** downloads the newer
  release, checks its SHA-256, replaces the launcher and restarts, keeping
  saves, settings and game files. This is the first release with it, so
  this update is still a manual download.
- **Controls.** Keyboard driving on WASD by default, keys rebound in **F6 >
  CONTROLS**, controllers mapped in game (**MAP CONTROLLER**), and a
  controller chord, Back + Start, that opens the settings.
- **Presets for smaller PCs.** New installs start at **Low-spec 60**;
  **Balanced 40** paces evenly at a third of 120's CPU work. The launcher
  recommends a preset for your PC.
- **The Forza Horizon XE mod** installs from your own ModDB downloads in
  every launcher, plays its own save and leaves yours alone. Mods can now
  keep your save when they only change looks or sound.
- **Saves from Xenia** import under their profile with
  `tools/import-xenia-save.ps1`.
- **Extracted game folders** are accepted when every file matches the
  supported disc.

## Fixed

- **Overexposed mornings above 1x**: the screen was too bright until about
  noon in game at 2x and up (#444).
- **A user folder with accents** or other non-ASCII characters, such as
  `C:\Users\Júnior`, closed the game at once (#436).
- **Custom Upgrade** crashed after loading a saved car.
- **A lost graphics device** now shows an explanation instead of the game
  vanishing, and a GPU without Vulkan 1.3 is explained before a build.
- **Setup** checks free space, the download sites, the install path, the
  Visual Studio installation and the Windows SDK before building, and
  resumes after a restart Visual Studio asks for.
- Two crashes in the game's own object handling: a dispatch through freed
  objects and an append into a corrupt list.
- The launchers' **Logs** link opens the game's own log once it has run
  (#424).

## Faster

- **Quad lists become triangle lists** on every platform: about 3 % of the
  GPU's frame at 2x on an RTX 4080 and 5 % on an M4 Pro.
- **TEXTURE GAMMA > FAST** on desktop saves about 8 % of the GPU's frame on
  a Mac, for slightly darker terrain and foliage.
- Cheaper draw bindings and system constants on the GPU recorder thread.

## Known limitations

- DLC import and the title update build are Windows only (Android gets them
  from the PC); Linux and macOS don't have them yet.
- The Steam Deck's settings menu didn't open for one player from the pause
  menu (#429). This build logs why; Back + Start opens it too.
- AMD and Intel GPUs on PCs are not qualified yet; the Steam Deck is.
  Racing wheels aren't supported (#337).
- The immersive camera isn't available on Android or the v4 build.
